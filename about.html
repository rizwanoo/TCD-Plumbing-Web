How We Work

Honest scope, tidy tools, and plain-English updates.

Everyday domestic plumbing jobs often matter most to how smoothly a household runs: a tap that refuses to turn off, a cistern constantly trickling into the bowl, or bathroom sealant that needs stripping and renewing cleanly.

TCD Plumbing serves homeowners, residential property owners, landlords and local tenants across Pontypridd, Caerphilly, Cardiff and Church Village. We focus strictly on general domestic plumbing repairs, replacements and maintenance—meaning you always get clear scope upfront and never get booked for work outside our verified remit.

  • 01

    Transparent scope & qualifications

    We believe in complete honesty with every customer: TCD Plumbing focuses on general domestic repairs and maintenance (not gas, boiler or central heating installation) and never exaggerates credentials or experience.

  • 02

    Built for homeowners & landlords

    Whether it is your own kitchen sink or a tenanted property in Cardiff or Caerphilly, we keep appointments clear and share updates directly by phone or email.

  • 03

    Affordable small-job focus

    Tap swaps, waste replacements, toilet repairs and bathroom resealing are never treated as an inconvenience—they are the core of what we do.
